Endless runner

No Exit

Synopsis
No Exit is a Unity endless runner. The player runs forward along a road that keeps generating, switches lanes, jumps obstacles, and picks up temporary effects such as a speed boost, a ghost shield, and a torch that clears fog. Two levels alternate as the run goes on, bosses appear later in the run, and the best score is saved and shown on the stats screen.
Project brief
This was a Game Development project to create an endless runner.
My role
I was the main coder for this game.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I coded the run itself: the road that keeps generating, lane switching, jumps, and the temporary pickups. The two levels take turns as the score climbs, bosses come in later, and the stats screen keeps the best score.

Puzzle

Dreamy Mosaics

Synopsis
Dreamy Mosaics is a Unity puzzle game. Each level is a Queens-style board: place one memory in every row, column, and coloured region, with no two memories touching. Illegal moves are explained and cost nothing. A legal move that is not part of the solution costs a heart, and undo gives that heart back.
Project brief
This was a Game Design project, made as a group.
My role
I was the main coder.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I coded the board rules. A memory has to sit alone in its row, column, and coloured region. The game explains an illegal move and leaves the hearts alone. A legal move that is not the solution costs a heart, and undo gives that heart back.

Game jam

Hex Mind

Synopsis
You are a necromancy witch trapped in a dungeon. The doors will not open for you. Near a locked door, take over the skeleton bound to it and walk that skeleton onto the pressure plate. The door swings open while the plate is held down.
Project brief
This was made at a short game jam where the theme was Hive Mind.
My role
I was the main coder.
Technologies
Unity and C#.
Process
I coded the possession. The witch cannot open a locked door herself. Near that door she takes over the skeleton bound to it, walks the skeleton onto the pressure plate, and the door stays open while the plate is held down.
